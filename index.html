The Lantern on the Mist-Cliff

A Cévennes mountain legend about greed, fear, and mercy.

Click the lantern to begin, then move through the tale.

INTRO — The Mist-Cliff Warning

In the Cévennes mountains, villagers whisper of a pale lantern that appears when greed blinds a traveler in the fog.

This is the night Marc learns that the White Lady of the pass does not reward stolen fortune—she tests the soul that carries it.

FIRST — Exposition

Characters: Marc, a desperate traveler fleeing with stolen coin, and the White Lady, a silent spirit guarding the pass.

Setting: A slippery mountain valley in medieval France on a bitter autumn night choked with fog.

Conflict: Marc must reach town before dawn to keep his wealth, but the mist hides the ravine edge and traps him high on the mountain.

NEXT — Rising Action

Phantom winds whisper through the trees as night deepens, and Marc loses the trail entirely.

His heavy purse drags him toward steep drops, yet greed keeps him from turning back.

Then a glowing white lantern appears ahead. Believing a villager waits in the fog, Marc hurries after the light.

SUDDENLY — Climax

The mist parts as Marc steps forward: he is standing on the absolute edge of a sheer cliff.

The lantern belongs to the White Lady. She does not shove him; she points to his stolen gold and demands a choice.

Marc finally understands. He drops the purse into the abyss. Freed from its weight, he stumbles back as the spirit bows and guides him away.

LAST — Resolution

The White Lady lights a clear ghostly path through the dark woods and leads Marc safely to the valley floor.

He reaches the village alive but penniless, changed forever by what greed nearly cost him.

To this day, locals say the White Lady appears in Cévennes fog: guiding the humble home, and forcing the greedy to drop their burdens into the ravine.
